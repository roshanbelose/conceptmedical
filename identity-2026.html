About Identity 2026

The Concept Leadership Conclave brings our leaders together to learn, reflect and recognise.

Identity 2026 is the annual leadership convention of the Concept Medical Group — three days in Goa dedicated to sharpening how we lead, how we work across functions, and how we hold ourselves to the standards our clinicians and patients depend on.

It pairs structured leadership development with an honest look at the year behind us — team presentations from every function, an open house on the challenges teams actually face, and a Rewards & Recognition evening that puts our people on stage.

The Theme

Up & Beyond

Up is direction — raising the standard of how we lead, decide and deliver. Beyond is distance — moving past the boundaries of our own function, our own market, our own last best year. Three days on the Goan coast, with the horizon in view, to do both.

Pillar 01

Leadership Enhancement

Communication, delegation, ownership and risk — taught as working practice, not theory, across two full programme days.

Pillar 02

Rewards & Recognition

Fifteen award categories honouring the individuals, teams and departments who moved the group forward this year.

Pillar 03

Target vs Achievement

An unvarnished review of commitments made and results delivered, presented by the heads of every function.

Leadership Message
Dr. Manish Doshi
Founder & Managing Director Concept Medical Group

"Converting concepts into reality." That has been our work since 2008, and it has never been the work of one person.

This year, our science stood on the world stage, and the world is now asking us to deliver at a scale we have never attempted. Every delay is a cost — to a hospital, to a clinician, to a patient waiting for a therapy that works. That urgency is our identity. Over these two days, speak plainly, listen closely, and leave with one commitment you will be measured against when we meet next year.

Leadership Message
Parth Doshi
Executive Director Concept Medical Group

"Let's Make History." Not a slogan — it's a decision we make every day. History is created when every individual, every function, and every action move in the same direction, with the same purpose.

Over these two days, all of us will sit in one room. Every function will put its identity on the table — what it owns, what it needs, and where it depends on the team beside it. Break the silos, understand each other's constraints, and leave aligned behind one shared vision. Extraordinary results are never achieved by individuals working harder; they are achieved by teams working together — up, and beyond.

Let's return united, accountable, and ready to make history, together.
