MagicTouch SCB

The coronary Sirolimus Coated Balloon

The world's first sirolimus-coated balloon for coronary artery disease — built on Concept Medical's Nanolute technology and CE marked, with extensive commercial use across Europe, Asia and the Middle East.

  • Indicated for in-stent restenosis, small vessels, bifurcations and de-novo lesions
  • World's first sirolimus-coated balloon — CE marked and commercially established
  • US FDA IDE approval for the In-Stent Restenosis (ISR) indication
  • 100,000+ patients treated; evidence from EASTBOURNE, TRANSFORM 1 & 2, NANOLUTE, GINGER

The science

Engineered for controlled delivery

Nanolute is Concept Medical's proprietary sirolimus delivery platform. By formulating the drug as sub-micron particles within a biocompatible carrier, it's designed to reach the vessel wall and act as a reservoir for sustained, controlled release.

  • Polymer-free nanocarriers of sirolimus in a phospholipid drug carrier
  • Sirolimus — a limus drug with a well-characterised safety profile
  • A deep, growing body of global coronary clinical evidence
