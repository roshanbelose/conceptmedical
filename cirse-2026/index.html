The science

Engineered for controlled delivery

Nanolute is Concept Medical's proprietary sirolimus delivery platform. By formulating the drug as sub-micron particles within a biocompatible carrier, it's designed to reach the vessel wall and release in a sustained, controlled manner.

  • Proven coronary heritage extended to peripheral applications
  • Sirolimus — a limus drug with a well-characterised safety profile
  • A growing body of global clinical evidence
