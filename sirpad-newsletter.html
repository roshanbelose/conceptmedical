Concept Medical announces the publication of the primary results of the Sirolimus-coated balloon for Peripheral Artery Disease (SirPAD) trial in the New England Journal of Medicine after presentation as a Late-Breaking Clinical Trial at ACC.26.

In the SirPAD randomized trial, the world's largest of its kind, angioplasty with MagicTouch PTA sirolimus-coated balloon was both noninferior and superior compared to uncoated balloons for the primary one-year outcome of major adverse limb events.

Key Highlights

"We are excited about the primary outcome results of SirPAD. It is one of the very few trials in this field that successfully included an all-comers population, meaning consecutive patients without eligibility restrictions related to target-lesion characteristics or PAD stage. With nearly 50% of enrolled patients having acute or chronic limb-threatening ischemia and 30% of patients with below-the-knee PAD, we could demonstrate superiority for hard clinical outcomes at one year. Moreover, we have shown an overall reduction of TLR."

Dr. Manish Doshi
Founder & Managing Director, Concept Medical

"SirPAD is an important milestone for the PAD community. This level of evidence reinforces our belief that meaningful innovation must be validated through rigorous science."
